About Me

Bonswa! My name is Roodcharle Filias. I'm a music producer, composer, audio and recording engineer, as well as a recording artist living in Brooklyn, New York. I am better known by my stage name, Not Today Roo.

I have been producing music since 2015. I started out on mobile apps and later transitioned into using LMMS and later into Fl Studio and Pro Tools. I taught myself how to produce, sing, and mix my own music, as well as for others. I have often collaborated with other artists, mainly my friends, and I often take the role of lead producer and head engineer of their respective projects.

I believe music is a powerful tool for expression and connection, and I strive to create music that can be shared with and resonate with others. I see music as the language of the soul, and when we take the time to understand and nurture it, only then can it truly flourish. I try to create music that reflects the experiences of myself and those around me. I enjoy telling stories through my music and creating new characters who have a wide array of personalities.

I have written and produced music for various genres, including hip-hop, R&B, pop, classical, and cinematic music. I enjoy blending genres together and creating my own sound. I am always looking for new ways to push the boundaries of creativity set by the music industry as well as myself.

I am currently studying music at CUNY Hunter College, and I have previously interned at Manhattan Beach Recording Studios, where I was given the chance to work with not only professional artists but also industry audio engineers, and I was given the chance to learn from some of the greats.

I have worked with a variety of artists, such as Santan Dave, Lazer Dim 700, Skrilla, OT7 Quanny, Xaviersobased, and BLP Kosher (to name a few).

Lately, I've been looking to get into post-production and more collaborative projects and maybe sync licensing. To be completely honest, I genuinely don't know where to start, and that's the beauty of music and audio. You just have to persevere through the unknown and just do it.

Feel free to contact me if you'd like to work, or just in general!

Ciao,
Roo
